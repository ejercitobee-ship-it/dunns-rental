# MH Dunn Property Management App

### A plain overview for the owner

**Prepared by:** MH Dunn Property
**Website:** mhdunnproperty.net


## What it is

A private web application that runs the rental business in one place. It replaces spreadsheets and scattered notes with a single, secure system for properties, tenants, rent, maintenance, and finances. It works on a computer or a phone, and it is hosted on a fast, secure platform with its own web address at mhdunnproperty.net.

The people who use it fall into two groups. The office manages everything from an admin view. Tenants, realtors, and handymen each get their own limited self service login that shows only what concerns them.


## What the office can do

**Dashboard.** A single screen showing income for the month, how many units are occupied, and how much rent is still owed.

**Properties and units.** Every building and unit, with occupancy, asking rent, and details. Properties now collapse into tidy summaries so the list stays clean as the portfolio grows.

**Tenants.** Each person, their household, their lease term, and their contact details, with a clear record of who lives where.

**Rent management.** Track what is owed and what has been collected, record payments, and generate a receipt automatically for each payment. A master rent spreadsheet mirrors to Google Sheets for easy sharing.

**Finances.** Income and expenses in one view, including net income and a per property breakdown. Rent collected and paid maintenance flow in automatically, so the numbers always agree with the dashboard.

**Maintenance.** A full repair workflow, described below.

**Reports and tax.** A current rent roll, a list of overdue balances, and a tax report for the year.

**Vendors and team.** Staff, realtors, and handymen are managed with clear roles, so each person can do their job and nothing more.

**Settings.** Company identity, payment instructions, and the connection to the company Google Drive where documents live.


## The tenant experience

Tenants receive a branded email invitation and set their own password. Once in, a tenant can:

- See their lease, their unit, and how to pay rent, including Zelle instructions.
- View their full rent history and download a receipt for any payment.
- See their documents.
- Report a maintenance issue, attach a photo, and say when they are home.
- Keep their own details current, including adding an emergency contact.

Tenants only ever see their own information.


## The maintenance workflow

This is the newest and largest part of the system. It connects tenants, handymen, and the office end to end:

1. A tenant reports an issue from their portal and can attach a photo.
2. Handymen who work that trade are notified, and the office is copied.
3. A handyman claims the job, or the office assigns it.
4. The handyman confirms a visit time from the tenant's availability.
5. The handyman marks the work in progress, then complete.
6. The office records what was paid, which is filed as an expense in Finances.

Handymen have their own portal with three tabs: available jobs in their trades, jobs in progress, and completed jobs. They can also keep their own profile, photo, and contact details current. The office never asks a tenant to pay a handyman. The office records that payment, and it never touches the tenant's rent.


## Realtors

Realtors who place tenants get their own portal showing the tenants they placed and the units available to market, along with their own profile. They are managed alongside handymen as business partners.


## Communication, documents, and security

**Email.** Professional, branded emails go out from info@mhdunnproperty.net for invitations, rent receipts, and maintenance updates.

**Documents and receipts.** Documents and auto generated rent receipts are stored in the company Google Drive, organized into folders.

**Security.** Every login is secured, and access is limited by role. Tenants, realtors, and handymen can only reach their own records. The office controls who can sign in and what each person can do.

**Hosting and email service.** The app runs on Cloudflare with a secure certificate at mhdunnproperty.net. Business email runs on Google Workspace, and the two work side by side without interfering.


## In short

Everything needed to run the rentals lives in one secure place: the properties, the people, the money, and the maintenance. Tenants, realtors, and handymen each get a simple self service window into exactly what they need, and the office keeps a clear, current picture of the whole operation.
