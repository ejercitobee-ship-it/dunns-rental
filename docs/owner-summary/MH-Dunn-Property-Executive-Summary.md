# MH Dunn Property

## Rental Management Platform

### Executive Summary

**Prepared for:** Landlords and Shareholders
**Platform:** mhdunnproperty.net
**Date:** July 2026


## Executive overview

MH Dunn Property operates a private, purpose-built software platform that runs the entire rental operation in one secure place. It manages the properties, the residents, the rent, the maintenance, and the finances, and it gives every party a clear, current view of exactly what concerns them.

The platform replaces spreadsheets, scattered notes, and the fees of an outside management company with a single system the business owns outright. It is live today at mhdunnproperty.net, works on any computer or phone, and installs on a resident's phone like a normal app.


## The problem it solves

Running a growing portfolio through spreadsheets and text messages breaks down quickly. Rent status becomes unclear, receipts go missing, maintenance falls through the cracks, and no one has a single trustworthy picture of the money.

The usual alternative is to hand the work to a third-party property manager, which typically costs 8 to 10 percent of all rent collected, every month, indefinitely. For a portfolio of this size that is a meaningful and permanent drag on returns.

This platform delivers that operational backbone directly, at effectively no per-transaction cost, while keeping full ownership of the data, the resident relationship, and the brand.


## What the platform delivers today

**Rent collection and financial clarity.** The system tracks what every lease owes and what has been collected, records each payment, and generates a professional receipt automatically. Rent is now shown to residents as due on a set day each month, and an automatic reminder goes out that morning by app notification and email. Any tenancy that falls behind is flagged automatically once it passes a threshold the office controls. Income and expenses reconcile into one financial view, with a per property breakdown, so the numbers always agree.

**The resident experience.** Each resident receives a branded invitation and their own secure login. They can see their lease and unit, view their full payment history, download any receipt, read how to pay, report a maintenance issue with a photo, and keep their own contact and emergency details current. Residents only ever see their own information. The experience is personal and branded, not a faceless portal.

**Maintenance, handled end to end.** A resident reports an issue, the right handyman for that trade is notified, and the office is kept in the loop. A handyman claims or is assigned the job, confirms a visit time around the resident's availability, and marks the work in progress and then complete. The office records what was paid, which files automatically as an expense. Residents are never asked to pay a handyman, and maintenance costs never touch rent.

**Oversight and accountability.** A dashboard shows monthly income, occupancy, and outstanding rent at a glance. Every action taken inside the system by staff, including recording a payment or updating a resident's record, is written to a permanent activity log. Ownership always has a complete and honest audit trail.

**Records and documents.** Receipts and documents are stored in the company Google Drive, organized into folders, with a master rent record that mirrors to a shareable spreadsheet. Nothing important lives on one person's laptop.


## Access, roles, and control

The office runs everything from a full administrative view. Everyone else gets a narrow, self-service login scoped to their role: residents, realtors who place tenants, and handymen who perform repairs. Access is invitation only. There is no public sign up, and the office controls exactly who can sign in and what each person is permitted to do.


## Security and reliability

Every login is protected with modern password security, and every account is confined to its own role and its own records. The platform has been through a dedicated security review, and resident, realtor, and handyman accounts have been verified to reach only their own data.

The application is hosted on Cloudflare, the same global infrastructure that serves many of the world's largest sites, with a valid security certificate at mhdunnproperty.net. Business email runs on Google Workspace, and transactional messages are authenticated so they arrive reliably and cannot be easily spoofed.


## Built to scale

The platform is designed to grow with the portfolio. It comfortably handles the current book of properties and units and has ample room to expand well beyond it, with no change in cost per property and no new software to buy. New properties, units, residents, and vendors are added directly, and the resident and vendor experiences scale automatically.


## On the roadmap

The next major step under evaluation is integrated online rent payment, allowing residents to pay by secure bank transfer with optional autopay. Because the platform already owns the lease records, receipts, and financial views, an integrated payment would let rent collect and reconcile itself automatically, further reducing manual work and speeding collection, while keeping fees far below the cost of card processing or a management company.


## The bottom line

Everything required to run the rentals lives in one secure system the business owns: the properties, the people, the money, and the maintenance. Residents and vendors each get a simple, branded window into exactly what they need, ownership keeps a complete and current picture of the whole operation, and the recurring cost of an outside management layer is avoided. The platform is live, in daily use, secure, and ready to scale.
